Paya Privacy Policy

Effective 14 November 2026 · Last updated 14 November 2026

Paya is a training, nutrition and health-tracking app for iPhone and Apple Watch. This policy explains what we collect, why, who else receives it, how long we keep it, and the choices you have. We wrote it to be read, not skimmed past.

Plain-language summary. Your data lives on your device first. If you create an account, a backup copy is stored in our cloud database so you can restore it on a new phone. We do not sell your data, we do not show ads, and we do not use third-party analytics or tracking. Paya is a wellness tool, not a medical device.

3. Where your data is stored

  • On your device (always): all data above is stored locally with Apple's SwiftData. The app works without an account.
  • In our cloud (only if you sign in): a backup copy of your profile and logs is stored in a Supabase (PostgreSQL) database, in the region configured for our project, protected by row-level security so each account can read only its own rows. Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest. Progress photos stay on your device unless a future version asks you to back them up.
  • Your sign-in tokens are stored in the iOS Keychain.

5. Legal bases (EEA / UK)

We process data to perform our contract with you (running the app and your account), based on your consent for health data, location, photos, voice and external AI (you can withdraw at any time), and for our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure.

6. Health data

Health information is sensitive. We use it only to provide the features you see. We never use it for advertising, and never share it with data brokers, advertisers or insurers. You control access under iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Health.

7. Retention

Local data stays until you delete it or delete the app. Cloud data stays while your account exists. When you delete your account (Settings → Account → Delete Account) we permanently delete your account and all your rows in our database and any stored files; routine backups expire within 30 days.

8. Your rights and choices

  • Access, correct, export: view and edit your data in the app; use Export in Settings.
  • Delete: remove entries individually, or delete your account in the app (immediate), or email us.
  • Withdraw consent: revoke Health, Location, Camera, Microphone, Notifications in iOS Settings; turn off "Allow External AI" in Paya Settings; remove AI keys.
  • Regional rights: if you are in the EEA/UK you may also object, restrict processing, request portability, and complain to your data-protection authority. California residents may request disclosure and deletion; we do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined.

Email eminhuseyn313@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

9. Medical disclaimer

Paya provides general wellness and fitness information. Readiness scores, flare-risk estimates, forecasts, AI answers, supplement and nutrition information are statistical estimates from your own data and general knowledge — they are not a diagnosis, treatment, or medical advice and may be wrong. Always consult a qualified clinician about symptoms, medication or health decisions. If you think you have a medical emergency, call your local emergency number.

10. Security

We use TLS in transit, encryption at rest, per-account access controls, and Keychain storage for credentials. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affecting you occurs we will notify you and regulators as the law requires.

11. Children

Paya is not intended for children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

12. International transfers

Providers above may process data in countries other than yours, including the United States. Where required, transfers rely on standard contractual clauses or equivalent safeguards.

13. Changes

If we make material changes we will update the date above and notify you in the app before they take effect.
